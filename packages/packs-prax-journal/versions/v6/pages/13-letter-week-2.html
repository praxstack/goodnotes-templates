weekly letter · week two of four
Dear you,

Whatever spark the first week had, it has probably gone quiet by now. The second week is where the new month stops feeling new and starts feeling like all the other ones.

You might be looking around for proof that something shifted, and finding none. That isn't a sign you did it wrong. The early days of anything run on novelty, and novelty was always going to wear off around here.

This is the part nobody photographs — the middle, where it's just the doing, without the freshness to carry it. It's allowed to feel like nothing. Feeling like nothing is not the same as nothing happening.

You don't have to manufacture momentum you don't have. You don't have to feel the second week to be inside it. Showing up flat still counts as showing up.

The quiet stretch isn't the month failing. It's just the month, continuing.

— the kit
