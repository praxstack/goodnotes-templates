§ doorway C · before it got heavy

What did the version of you at 18 or 20 — before all of this —
actually love doing?

1.

Not what you were good at. What you enjoyed. Without marks. Without comparison.

2.

Where were you when you did it? Who was around, or were you alone?

↓
3.

When did you stop, and what made you stop? Be specific if you can.

4.

Is there one piece of that, however small, that could exist again — not as a project, just as a presence?

grief for a younger self is real. let it land. don't fix it.
