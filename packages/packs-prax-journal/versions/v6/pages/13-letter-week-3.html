weekly letter · week three of four
Dear you,

This is usually the week where quitting starts to sound reasonable. Not dramatic — just quietly sensible, like the obvious thing a tired person would do.

I'm not going to tell you it gets easier from here, because I don't know that, and you'd hear the lie. The third week is often just hard. The reasons to stop are real reasons. I'm not going to pretend they aren't.

So I'll only say this: you can put the decision down for tonight. You don't have to quit and you don't have to recommit. You can leave the whole question alone until you're less worn down than you are right now.

If today all you did was not make it worse, that was a full day's work. The wall doesn't have to be climbed in one move. You're allowed to just stand near it.

You're still here, reading this. That's not nothing — that's the hardest part, happening.

— the kit
