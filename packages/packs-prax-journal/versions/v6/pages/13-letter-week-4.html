weekly letter · week four of four
Dear you,

The month is closing. You don't have to add it up. There's no column at the bottom of this one, no number it resolves into, no grade you owe yourself before it's allowed to end.

Some of these days you'd want back. Some of them disappeared without your say-so. A few were probably fine in a way that's hard to remember now. All of it still counts as the month you lived.

If you're tempted to call the whole stretch wasted, you can set that down. A month isn't a test you passed or failed. It's just time you moved through — and you did move through it, which is the only thing it ever asked.

You don't have to carry this one into the next. You can let it close the way a day closes: without ceremony, without a score, just done.

You were here for all of it. That's the whole report. The next month will keep, until you're ready for it.

— the kit
