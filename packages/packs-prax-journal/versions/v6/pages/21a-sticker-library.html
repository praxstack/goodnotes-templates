cut · peel · stamp on demand

forty-one stickers across ten packs. you don't earn them. you stamp them when the moment is true. blank entries also count.

packs: restlessness · break-discipline · wins-jar · mood-constellation · cbt-thought-flip · dbt-urge-surf · pattern-names · permission · if-then · crisis-toolkit

01

RESTLESSNESS-NAMED

"the bee in the hat" — when the body says go and you wrote one word for it.

02

URGE-SURFED

"the wave passed" — the urge came, peaked, and left without you acting on it.

03

PSEUDO-ACTION-CAUGHT

"it wasn't doing" — the move that wasn't a move. caught before the doing.

04

PHONE-REACH-NOTICED

"the hand in mid-air" — you noticed before the hand landed on the screen.

05

BODY-FELT

"the chest knew first" — a 2-second pause registered. one body-word logged.

06

BREAK-TAKEN

"the chair was empty" — you took the break — not "should have," took it.

07

BREAK-RETURNED

"came back on time" — timer ran, you came back. of your own accord.

08

OFF-RAMP-USED

"closed the laptop" — the close was on purpose, not by collapse.

09

EMPTY-CUP-REFILLED

"literally" — you refilled the cup before it ran fully dry.

10

WIN-STAMPED

"small but real" — any size win. "got out of bed" qualifies. small counts.

11

STREAK-CONTINUED

"one more day" — one more day on the chain. no breaking, no shame.

12

SURPRISED-MYSELF

"didn't expect that" — you did a thing that surprised you. logged for evidence.

13

HEAVY-BUT-HERE

"showed up anyway" — the heaviness was real and you came anyway.

14

LIGHT-AND-AWARE

"caught the lift" — the lift was real too. caught it on the way up.

15

NUMB-ACKNOWLEDGED

"no spin" — the day was flat and you wrote that, not a story about it.

16

CATASTROPHE-CAUGHT

"I named it" — the worst-case story arrived. you named it as a story.

17

FILTER-SPOTTED

"saw what I was missing" — you saw what your filter was filtering out.

18

FORTUNE-TOLD-RETURNED

"came back from the future" — you left the future, came back to now.
