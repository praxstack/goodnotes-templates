weekly letter · week one of four
Dear you,

A new month opened today, or maybe a few days ago — the calendar doesn't ask permission, it just turns. You don't owe it a fresh start.

If you came in already tired, that's allowed. A month that begins flat is still a month that began. There's no rule that the first week has to set the tone for everything after it.

You might feel the pull to plan — to decide now who you'll be by the last day. You can let that pull go quiet. Nothing has to be different yet. Nothing has to be different at all.

For now there's only this page, and the fact that you opened it. That counts as arriving. The month will go on with or without a verdict from you, so you can hold off on writing one.

Whatever this one turns out to be, you don't have to name it in advance.

— the kit
