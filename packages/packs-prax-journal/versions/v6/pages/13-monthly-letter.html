Dear Prax,

A month is a long time to carry something. If this one was heavy, it doesn't need a name yet. If it was uneven — some good days, some that disappeared — that's also a real month.

You probably told yourself this would be the month you finally got it together. Most months arrive carrying that hope. Most leave without making good on it. That isn't failure. That's how the year is shaped — in soft, mostly uneventful chapters that you only see clearly later.

If there's a sentence you've been carrying — I should be further along by now — you can put it down here. Not forever. Just for the time it takes to read this page. The sentence will still be there when you get up. It just doesn't have to follow you across this paragraph.

You don't have to do anything with this letter. You don't have to underline a part. You don't have to write back. You don't have to feel something specific in response.

If you want one small thing: notice what your shoulders are doing right now. You can let them down a half-inch and keep reading. Or you can close the page. Either is the right move.

Whatever this month was — you made it here. That's the whole report.

— the kit.
